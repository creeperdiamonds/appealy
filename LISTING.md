__***Applications, ban appeals and tickets, in one bot***__

Appealy turns your server's forms into a real review queue. Members apply from a panel button or `/apply`, staff review each submission on the web dashboard, and one click does the rest — roles granted, DM sent, member unbanned.

**Applications 📋**

- Build forms with your own questions on the web dashboard
- Publish them as panel buttons, or let members use `/apply`
- Custom outcomes: accept, deny, "trial moderator", anything you like — each with its own roles, DM and log channel
- Cooldowns, editable DM templates, and staff review rights without handing out Administrator

**Ban appeals 🔓**

- Banned members get a DM telling them what happened, with a button to appeal if they want to
- Appeals land in the same review queue as everything else
- Accepting one unbans them automatically

**Tickets 🎫**

- Ticket panels with their own support roles per ticket type
- Transcripts and ticket ratings

**Also included:**

Giveaways · polls · verification · welcome messages · role menus · sticky messages · anti-raid join detection

**Why Appealy**

- Every feature is free. Paid plans raise usage limits — they never unlock features.
- Open source (AGPL-3.0) and free to self-host
- No lock-in: `/export` gives you your server's data in one JSON file
- Coming from Appy? `/import-appy` brings your applications with you
