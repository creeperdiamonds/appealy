Plans and payment

  • Billing is annual. There is no monthly option, anywhere. The reasoning is set out in full on the pricing page and in the source. Monthly figures shown anywhere on this site are computed from the annual price for comparison and are never a cadence you can select.
  • Tebex is the merchant of record. It sells to you, collects payment, and handles sales tax and VAT where it sells. Payment questions, invoices and refunds go through Tebex.
  • Paid plans are annual subscriptions. A plan that stops being paid for returns the server to the free selection. A refunded payment does the same.
  • A plan is only applied after the amount actually paid has been checked against what that plan costs, currency included. A mismatch is refused rather than applied at the wrong price.
  • Downgrading to the fully free selection never involves a checkout, because there is no payment to confirm.
  • A selection totalling less than $5.00 a year is refused at checkout rather than billed.

Caps and ceilings

Every metered cap has a hard ceiling, including the ones you set yourself. There is no unlimited tier and there will not be one. A request above a ceiling is refused and names the field that is too high, rather than being silently clamped to a number you did not choose.

Exceeding your cap does not delete anything. It stops the action that would cross the line until the window resets or the cap is raised.

Platform bans, and appealing one

A user or a server can be banned from using the hosted bot — for abuse of the service, or for using it to do something Discord forbids. This is separate from and unrelated to a ban inside your own Discord server; guild staff cannot see platform bans, and a platform ban is not something your server’s appeal flow handles.

A platform ban can be appealed. The dashboard shows a banned user the reason and a form to contest it. A product whose mark is an open door does not get to make its own bans final.

Availability

There is no uptime guarantee. The status page is deliberately a static file that does not depend on the API or the database, so it keeps working during the outage it is reporting. It says nothing when the gateway stops reporting in, and after a minute of silence it says so plainly rather than showing a stale green.
