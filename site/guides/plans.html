Plans and upgrading

Every feature is free. Paid plans only raise limits, like how many applications or tickets a server can get per day.

Paid plans aren’t on sale yet. Appealy is still waiting for its payment provider to approve it, so nobody can be charged and you don’t need to upgrade to use anything. This page will say so when it changes.

See your limits

  1. Open Billing

    In the dashboard, click Billing (under Administration).

  2. Look at Current plan

    The table shows each limit, how much your server uses now, and what the free plan allows. Most servers never get near them.

  3. When plans are on sale

    Under Change plan you pick a plan, check the price under What this costs, and press Continue to checkout. Your plan changes once the payment goes through.

Prices and the full list of limits are on the pricing page.

Stuck on a step? Ask in the support server, or see all guides.
