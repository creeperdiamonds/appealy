Refund Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Fourteen days to change your mind, no reason required. This page is the detail behind the “Plans and payment” section of the Terms of Service; the two say the same thing, and if they ever disagree the Terms are what binds.

The window

You may ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. No reason is required inside that window — changing your mind is reason enough. Billing is annual, so this applies to the payment you made for that year, and equally to a renewal.

How to ask

Paddle is the merchant of record for Appealy. Paddle sells Appealy to you, collects the payment, and owes the sales tax and VAT on it — so a refund is asked of Paddle, and Paddle processes it. Your receipt comes from Paddle.

If you would rather start with us, email contact@creeperdiamonds.xyz and we will point you at the right place or ask on your behalf.

What happens to your data

Nothing is deleted. When a refund is processed the server returns to the free selection, and only the caps change back. Your forms, submissions, tickets and settings stay exactly where they are. If you buy again later, they are still there.

After 14 days

A plan runs to the end of the year it was bought for. You are not billed again unless the subscription renews, and cancelling stops the next renewal rather than ending the plan you already paid for — you keep what you bought until it runs out.

One exception, and we mean it: if something on our side is broken and that is why you want the money back, say so anyway. The window is a policy for changing your mind, not a shield against our own faults.

What is not refundable

The self-hosted software is free under the AGPL-3.0 and is never sold, so there is nothing to refund there. This page covers the hosted service at appealy.app only.

Questions

Email contact@creeperdiamonds.xyz, or ask in the community server. Billing questions about a specific payment are quickest through Paddle, the merchant of record, who holds the transaction and can act on it directly.
