Appealy is up

Appealy is running.

An open-source Discord bot for applications, tickets and ban appeals — built around the idea that a ban should never come with the sentence “this decision is final and cannot be appealed”.

Every feature is available on the free tier. Paid plans raise throughput caps and nothing else, and the calculator that decides what you are charged is in the repository along with everything else.
